← planetarypat.com · Field photos courtesy of the ANSMET program (Case Western Reserve University and the University of Utah), via the NASA JSC Antarctic Meteorite database. Earth rocks and most meteorites come from the Dominion Range (DOM) and nearby icefields: Miller Range (MIL), MacAlpine Hills (MAC), Lewis Cliff (LEW), Grosvenor Mountains (GRO) and Queen Alexandra Range (QUE). Irons, stony-irons, lunar and Martian meteorites come from every ANSMET icefield. This is an unofficial study aid, not an ANSMET or NASA product.
